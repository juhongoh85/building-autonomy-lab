01 · Background

Ju-Hong OhAssistant Professor · Ph.D. in Engineering

AI x for building autonomy

I combine physics-based models with operational data to extend the range of prediction, and interpret how buildings operate through physical cause and effect.

By training agents on a digital twin, I aim to realize autonomous operation in which buildings assess their own state and demand, make decisions, and control themselves.

Collaboration with Industry and Research Institutes

We welcome joint research on building operation diagnosis, performance improvement, and control strategies.
Topics for joint research and technical consulting are shaped around your data and site conditions.

Possible research topics

  • Physics- and data-based performance prediction models using limited operational data
  • Physics-informed interpretation of equipment performance, fault detection and diagnosis (FDD), and measurement and verification (M&V) of energy savings
  • Building and equipment digital twins: training and evaluating operating strategies, and reviewing field deployment
  • Integrated modeling and operating strategies for heat pump, thermal storage, and hydrothermal systems
  • Development of equipment design and performance evaluation programs
